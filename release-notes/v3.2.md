Claude can now organize your voice memos and Brain Dumps. Open Settings → AI Intelligence → Claude, paste your Anthropic API key, and choose Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 or Haiku 5.5. Requests are billed to your Claude Console account, and the key is encrypted on this device and left out of backups.

While Claude is on, it organizes captures and your ChatGPT settings are not used for organization. Turn Claude off to switch back. The voice conversation assistant still uses OpenAI.

Install from Settings → App updates.
